Session usage bars

The bar, repeated. One row per half hour, oldest at the top, the present at the bottom where the bar already sits. Percent stays on x in every row, so each one is the instrument people already read, and no axis moves. The clock mark becomes one line, from the window opening at the top left to where the clock stands now at the bottom right, masked through the rows it crosses at the notch's own thickness. No prediction line: the head row states the estimate instead.
